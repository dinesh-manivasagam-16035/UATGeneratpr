BRD: Lead sizing and priority follow-up
Product: Zoho CRM · Module: Leads
Prepared for: CRM implementation team

Purpose
Sales wants to know, the moment a lead arrives, how keen the lead is and how large the deal
could be — and to react to the keenest leads the same day. This document states what the CRM
must do.

Background
Today a new lead shows only a name and a source. Reps open every lead to find out whether it
is worth a call, hot leads wait in the same queue as everyone else, and leads are sometimes
saved with a seat count of zero, which breaks the sizing report.

Functional requirements

FR-1: Buying Interest
- Add a pick list "Buying Interest" to the Leads module with the values Hot, Warm and Cold.
- The field is optional and has no default value.

FR-2: Expected Seats
- Add a whole-number field "Expected Seats" to the Leads module: the number of users the lead
  expects to buy for.
- The field is optional.

FR-3: Priority follow-up for hot leads
- When a lead is created with Buying Interest "Hot":
  - set its Lead Status to "Priority Follow-up", and
  - create a task for the lead owner with the subject "Call within 4 hours", priority High,
    due the same day.
- "Priority Follow-up" is a new Lead Status value.
- A lead created as Warm or Cold keeps the Lead Status it was created with and gets no task.

FR-4: Expected Seats must be a real number of seats
- A lead must not be saved with Expected Seats less than 1.
- Show the message: "Expected Seats must be 1 or more."
- An empty Expected Seats is allowed.
- This must be enforced on the Leads form itself (a validation rule), not by an automation
  that runs after the save.

Acceptance criteria
A. Create a lead with Buying Interest "Hot" → Lead Status is "Priority Follow-up" and the lead
   owner has an open task "Call within 4 hours" with priority High.
B. Create a lead with Buying Interest "Warm" and Lead Status "Contacted" → Lead Status stays
   "Contacted" and no task is created.
C. Save a lead with Expected Seats 0 → the save is rejected with the message in FR-4.
D. Save the same lead with Expected Seats 25 → the save succeeds.

Out of scope
- Reports, dashboards and email notifications.
- Changes to Contacts, Accounts or Deals.
