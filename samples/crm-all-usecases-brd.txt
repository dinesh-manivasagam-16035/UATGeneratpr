BRD: Partner Channel Programme — Zoho CRM implementation
Client: Northwind Distribution Pvt Ltd
Modules in scope: Leads, Accounts, Contacts, Deals
Prepared for: CRM implementation team

Purpose
Northwind sells through partners. Partner leads arrive from the website, events and the
channel team, and are qualified, converted into accounts and taken through a deal cycle.
This document states what the CRM must do. It covers the data the team records, who may
see it, and the automation that keeps the process moving.

Background
Today the channel team keeps partner data in spreadsheets. Leads are lost between the
enquiry and the first call, nobody can tell which region owns an account, and the
quarterly partner report is assembled by hand. The CRM must become the single record.

Functional requirements

Data model

FR-1: Partner code on leads and accounts
- Record a "Partner Code" on both Leads and Accounts: free text, up to 20 characters.
- The code identifies a partner for the rest of its life and must never repeat: two
  accounts may not carry the same Partner Code.
- The field is optional while a lead is still an enquiry.

FR-2: Partner tier
- Classify every partner as Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum.
- The same four tiers are used on Leads, Accounts and Deals, and the channel team must
  be able to change the list in one place rather than in each module.

FR-3: Partner segment and sub-segment
- Record the partner's business segment: Retail, Manufacturing, Healthcare or Education.
- Each segment has its own sub-segments, and only the sub-segments of the chosen segment
  may be selected:
  - Retail: Grocery, Apparel, Electronics
  - Manufacturing: Automotive, Industrial, Packaging
  - Healthcare: Hospitals, Clinics, Pharma
  - Education: Schools, Colleges, Training

FR-4: Onboarding dates
- Record the "Partner Since" date on Accounts and the "Follow Up Date" on Leads.
- The channel team also records "Expected Annual Revenue" (currency) and the
  "Agreement Signed" flag (yes/no) on Accounts.

FR-5: Partner details on the account form
- The partner fields must appear together in a "Partner Programme" section of the
  Accounts form, not scattered among the standard fields.

FR-6: Partner onboarding checklist module
- Track onboarding tasks in a module of their own: "Partner Onboarding", with the task
  name, the account it belongs to, the owner, a due date and a status
  (Not Started, In Progress, Blocked, Done).

Access and visibility

FR-7: Channel roles
- Three roles report to the Sales Director: Channel Manager, Channel Executive and
  Partner Support.
- A Channel Executive may view, create and edit Leads, Accounts, Contacts and Deals, but
  may not delete any record. Partner Support may view Accounts and Contacts only.

FR-8: Regional territories
- Accounts belong to a region: North India, West India and South India sit under an
  India territory.
- An account belongs to West India when its billing state is Maharashtra, Gujarat or Goa.
- The regional Channel Manager owns the territory and the team may read and edit the
  accounts in it; they must not see the other regions' accounts.

FR-9: Support visibility of deals
- Partner Support must be able to read every Deal owned by the Channel Manager role and
  the roles below it, so that a support call can be answered without asking sales.
  Support must not be able to edit those deals.

Process and automation

FR-10: Flag enquiries without a partner code
- When a lead is created without a Partner Code, set its Lead Status to "Code Pending"
  so the channel team can see what to chase. "Code Pending" is a new status value.

FR-11: Qualification gate
- A lead must not be saved with Lead Status "Qualified" while the Partner Code is empty.
- The message shown is: "Enter the partner code before qualifying this lead."
- This must be enforced on the form itself, not by an automation that runs afterwards.

FR-12: Lead assignment
- Leads from the website go to the Channel Executive team in rotation; leads from an
  event go to the Channel Manager of the region named on the lead.

FR-13: Follow-up reminder
- Two business days before a lead's Follow Up Date, create a task for the lead owner:
  "Call the partner before the follow-up date", priority High.

FR-14: Stale enquiry escalation
- When a lead has been sitting in "Code Pending" 24 hours after it was created, set its
  Lead Status to "Escalated" and email the owner's manager using the existing
  "Lead Escalation" email template.

FR-15: Welcome email on agreement
- When Agreement Signed becomes yes on an Account, email the account owner and the
  partner's primary contact using the existing "Partner Welcome" template.

FR-16: Tier recalculation
- When the Expected Annual Revenue of an account changes, recalculate the Partner Tier:
  below 10 lakh is Bronze, up to 50 lakh is Silver, up to 2 crore is Gold, above that is
  Platinum. The rule must also count the revenue of deals won in the last twelve months,
  so a simple field comparison is not enough.

FR-17: Notify the partner portal
- Whenever an account's Partner Tier changes, notify the partner portal at
  https://portal.northwind.example.com/hooks/tier so it can refresh its pricing.

FR-18: Onboarding process
- A partner's onboarding runs through these stages in order: Enquiry, Documents
  Received, Agreement Signed, Onboarded. Moving forward is a deliberate step by the
  channel team, and the stage a partner is in must be visible on the account.

FR-19: Deal stages for the channel pipeline
- Channel deals run through their own stages: Partner Qualified, Solution Fit,
  Commercials, Closed Won and Closed Lost. The direct sales pipeline stays as it is.

FR-20: Quarterly partner review
- The channel team reviews partners every quarter. The review itself is run outside the
  CRM, but the CRM must hold the review date and outcome on the account.

Reporting and other requirements

FR-21: Channel dashboard
- The Channel Manager needs a dashboard showing enquiries by status, deals by stage and
  partners by tier.

FR-22: Partner agreement document
- The signed agreement is attached to the account. The team also sends a standard
  "Partner Agreement" letter, which must be available as a template in the CRM.

FR-23: Business hours
- Automation that counts business days must use the India working week: Monday to
  Friday, 09:30 to 18:30 IST, with the company's declared holidays excluded.

FR-24: Data migration
- 1,840 existing partner accounts and 4,200 contacts must be loaded from the current
  spreadsheets, with their Partner Codes preserved.

Acceptance criteria
A. A lead saved without a Partner Code shows Lead Status "Code Pending".
B. Saving a lead as "Qualified" with an empty Partner Code is rejected with the stated
   message.
C. Choosing segment "Retail" offers only Grocery, Apparel and Electronics as
   sub-segments.
D. A second account cannot be saved with a Partner Code that already exists.
E. A Channel Executive cannot delete an account; Partner Support cannot open a deal for
   editing.
F. An account whose billing state is Gujarat appears under the West India territory.
G. A lead left in "Code Pending" for 24 hours becomes "Escalated" and the owner's
   manager receives the escalation email.
H. Changing Expected Annual Revenue to 75,00,000 sets Partner Tier to Gold and the
   portal webhook is called.
I. A partner can be moved from Enquiry to Documents Received, but not straight to
   Onboarded.
J. A channel deal can be created in the Partner Qualified stage.
